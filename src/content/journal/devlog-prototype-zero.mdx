# Prototype Zero — The Brewing Begins

Every studio's first devlog is the same strange mix of excitement and uncertainty. Here's what the first BrewPine prototype looks like right now.

## The Concept

The prototype is a 2D platformer — but the movement is the experiment. Instead of standard left-right-jump controls, the player character *flows* — sticking to surfaces, sliding around corners, and building momentum in ways that feel slightly wrong in interesting ways.

The working title is **Project Mycelium**. Because everything is connected underground.

## What Exists So Far

- A movement controller in GDScript that handles surface gravity.
- A hand-drawn tileset (rough pencil art, no clean lines).
- Two test levels that feel more like sketches than levels.
- A camera system that follows loosely, with a slight delay.

```gdscript
# Current movement snippet — expects revision
func _physics_process(delta: float) -> void:
  var input := Input.get_vector("move_left", "move_right", "move_up", "move_down")
  velocity = velocity.lerp(input * SPEED, ACCELERATION * delta)
  move_and_slide()
```

This is rough. The acceleration feel needs work. The jump arc is inconsistent. But something interesting happens when you gain speed through a slope — that's the thing worth following.

## Open Questions

1. **Is this fun or just strange?** Unknown yet.
2. **Does the hand-drawn art style hold together at this scale?** Needs more tiles to know.
3. **What is the player trying to do?** Still unclear. The mechanic comes first, the goal comes later.

## What's Next

- More tile variety.
- A clearer design goal (something to reach, something to avoid).
- Better camera framing.
- Playtesting with someone who hasn't seen the controls before.

Updates coming when there's something worth showing.
