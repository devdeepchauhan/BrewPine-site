# Welcome to BrewPine

Something is brewing.

BrewPine is an independent game development studio — a creative workshop where experiments become prototypes, prototypes become games, and games become something strange and interesting.

The name comes from two ideas: **brewing**, which implies mixing, experimenting, and letting things simmer until they're ready — and **pine**, which brings to mind deep forests, quiet persistence, and things that stay green even when everything else changes.

## What This Studio Is

BrewPine is not a corporation. It's not a team of forty people with a roadmap and a PR department. It's a small, curious, independent initiative exploring:

- **Game mechanics** — what makes a game feel interesting to play?
- **Programming** — how do you build systems that surprise even you?
- **Art and storytelling** — what atmosphere, what world, what feeling?
- **Experimental projects** — things that might not work, but are worth trying.

## The Journal

This journal is where the experimentation gets documented. Development logs, technical breakdowns, tutorials, discoveries, and behind-the-scenes notes — all of it goes here.

Think of it as a field notebook: imperfect, honest, and full of things that are still being figured out.

## What's Coming

The studio is at the beginning. There are prototypes in progress, ideas being tested, and worlds being brewed. Some of them will become real games. Some will become tutorials. Some will just be interesting things to look at.

All of it will be documented here.

Welcome to BrewPine. Let's see what we can make.
